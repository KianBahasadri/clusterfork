About & help

Read it your way

PDF opens on most phones and computers. EPUB lets you adjust the text in a reading app. For the offline website, unzip the download and open grade-book/index.html. Keep its folder together.

This page is a design-guide snapshot. Its cover is a generated stand-in, and the archive rows below are sample entries on this one page. The download buttons open the upstream edition from 20 October 2026.
